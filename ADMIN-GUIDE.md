# Editor guide — updating the TCF Melbourne website

This guide is for church editors. You don't need any technical knowledge. Everything you change
shows on the website **straight away**. Visitors may need to refresh the page to see it.

---

## 1. Open the admin page

The admin page is **not linked anywhere on the website**. That's on purpose. Open it by its address:

> **https://tcfmelbourne.org/admin/**

**Bookmark it** the first time you open it:

- **Phone (iPhone):** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Phone (Android):** tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.
- **Computer:** press **Ctrl + D** (Windows) or **⌘ + D** (Mac).

Please don't share the address publicly. It's only for editors.

---

## 2. Log in

1. Type your **email** and **password**, then tap **Log in**.
2. You'll see three tabs at the top: **Events**, **Service times** and **Gallery**.
3. When you've finished, tap **Sign out** (top right), especially on a shared computer.

**Your first time:** you'll get an invitation email from the church administrator. Open the link in it,
choose a password, and then use the address above from then on.

**"Your login has expired"**: you were logged in for a long time, or logged out somewhere else.
Log in again. Anything you'd already saved is safe. Only an edit you hadn't saved yet needs to be typed again.

---

## 3. Add a prayer meeting (or any event)

1. Go to the **Events** tab and tap **Add event**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: e.g. *Prayer Meeting*. Titles are saved and shown in Title Case for you
     (*prayer meeting* becomes *Prayer Meeting*; short words such as *and*, *of*, *at* stay lowercase).
   - **Status**: leave it on **Normal**
   - **Type**: choose **Prayer** (or **Meeting** for other gatherings). On a Sunday it is set to
     **Sunday service** for you: the regular service, shown without a label. Its note (e.g.
     *Conducted by Youth / Sunday School Seniors*) appears as plain text under the time.
   - **Date**: tap the box and pick the day
   - **Time**: write it the way it should look, e.g. *7pm* or *from 6:30pm*
   - **Campus**: tick one or more campuses, or **All campuses**
   - **Note** (optional): a short extra line. If you start it with "with" it shows as a
     speaker line, e.g. *with Ps Pitchaiah (Guest Speaker, India)*
3. Check the **Preview on the website** box. That's exactly how it will look.
4. Tap **Save event**.

Each event in the list shows **who last changed it and when**.

**To change an event:** tap **Edit** next to it, change what you need, then tap **Save event**.

> Events on a **Sunday** appear in that Sunday's card. All other days appear under **Gatherings**.
> The website only shows events from today onwards (Melbourne time): an event disappears by itself the day
> after its date, so you don't need to delete it. It stays here under **Past events** (tap to open). Use
> **Copy** on a past event to add it again on a new date: the form opens with the same details and an
> empty **Date**, and the past event is left as it was.

## 4. Post a cancellation

Use this when a service or event **won't go ahead**.

1. **Events** tab → **Add event**.
2. **Status**: choose **Cancelled**.
3. **Date**: pick the day.
4. **Campus**: tick every campus that's cancelled.
   - On a Sunday, the **title** ("Sunday service") fills in for you. There is no **Time** to fill in:
     a cancelled service has none.
5. **Combined with another campus** (optional): if everyone joins another campus that day, choose
   it (e.g. Tarneit). Leave it on **Not combined** otherwise.
6. Add a **Note** if helpful, e.g. *Bring a plate to share*.
7. Tap **Save event**. A box asks you to confirm. It names the **campus** and **date** and shows a
   preview. Check them carefully, then tap **Yes, post cancellation**.

On the website:

- Each cancelled campus shows a **Cancelled** label without a time, then *Combined with Tarneit,
  4:30pm* if you chose a campus (with that campus's Sunday time), then your note.
- On a Sunday, a cancelled "Sunday service" **replaces** that campus's regular service for the day.
  Its usual row is hidden automatically, so you don't need to edit or delete it. In the admin list
  it says *Not shown on the website*.
- The campus everyone joins keeps its normal row, with *Includes the Melton and Mernda services*.
- It appears in the **"This week"** box on the home page, the Service times page and that campus's
  page during the 7 days before, and in the morning email.

**Already added the event?** Tap **Edit**, change **Status** to **Cancelled**, and save.

### Guest speakers and other one-off changes

- **A guest speaker:** leave **Status** on **Normal**, keep the type **Sunday service**, and write
  *with Ps Name (Guest Speaker, Country)* in the **Note**. It shows under the campus, without a label.
- **A different time or place this Sunday only:** choose **Service change** as the type. Only real
  changes like this show a **Service change** label.
- **A youth-led service:** keep **Sunday service** and write it in the note, e.g. *Conducted by
  Youth / Sunday School Seniors*.

### Adding a special event

Use this for an event at a place that **isn't one of our campuses**, e.g. *Christmas Night* at
TCF Ministries, Keilor East, or a children's fest at another church.

1. **Events** tab → **Add event**. **Title**: e.g. *Christmas Night*.
2. **Type**: choose **Special event**. **Campus** switches to **Not at a campus** for you.
3. **Date** and **Time**, e.g. *4pm*. Add an **End time** if you know it, e.g. *6pm*
   (shown as *4pm to 6pm*).
4. **Location**: the **Venue name** is required, e.g. *TCF Ministries*. Add the **Address** too,
   e.g. *Keilor East VIC*. The website uses it for a **Get directions** link to Google Maps.
   Only fill in **Map or info link** if you have a better link. It must start with `https://`.
5. **Note** (optional), e.g. *Kids program. All welcome.* Check the preview and tap **Save event**.

On the website it has a **Special event** label, the venue and address, and **Get directions**. It
shows on the home page, on the Events page under every campus, on every campus page, and in the
**"This week"** box in the week before. It never changes a Sunday service. The morning email lists
special events for the next 30 days.

## 5. Change a regular service time

Only do this when a campus's **regular, every-week** details change (for example a new venue,
or the service moves to 10am permanently).

> **For a one-off change on a single Sunday, add a Service change or Cancellation event
> instead of editing the regular time.**

1. Go to the **Service times** tab.
2. Find the campus and change what you need: **Regular Sunday time**, **Venue**, **Address**,
   **Transport note**, **Language** or **Map link**.
   - For the map link, open the place in Google Maps, tap **Share**, copy the link and paste it here.
     It must start with `https://`.
3. Tap **Save** (e.g. **Save Melton**) under that campus. A box shows what is changing (old → new). Tap **Save changes**.

The new details appear on every page of the website straight away.

## 6. Upload photos

> **Only upload photos of people who agreed to appear on the website, with extra care for children.**
> If in doubt, leave it out, or ask the parents first.

1. Go to the **Gallery** tab.
2. Tap **Choose photos** and pick one or more photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP).
3. For **each** photo, fill in:
   - **Describe the photo (alt text)**: **required**. Write what someone who can't see the
     photo would need to know, e.g. *Children singing at the Sunshine Christmas service*.
   - **Caption** (optional): shown under the photo.
   - **Campus**: the campus page it should appear on, or **All campuses**.
4. Tap **Upload photos**. Photos are made smaller automatically before uploading, so this is quick even on a phone.

**Change the order:** use **Move up** / **Move down**. The first photos are the ones shown on the home page.
**Fix a description or caption:** change it in the box and tap **Save**.

---

## 7. Delete something, and get it back

Nothing is lost straight away when you delete it.

- **Delete an event:** tap **Delete** and confirm. It disappears from the website.
  A message appears for a few seconds with an **Undo** button.
- **Delete a photo:** tap **Delete** under it and confirm.

**To restore:**

1. Open **Recently deleted** at the bottom of the **Events** or **Gallery** tab.
2. Tap **Restore** next to the item. It's back on the website straight away.

**Removing a photo completely:** if someone asks for their photo to be taken down, delete it, then open
**Recently deleted** and tap **Delete forever**. This removes the image file too and **can't be undone**.

---

## 8. Forgot your password / reset it

1. On the login page, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Type your email and tap **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email (check your **junk/spam** folder too) and tap the link. It works **once** and
   expires after about an hour.
4. Choose a new password (at least 10 characters) and type it twice. Tap **Save new password**.
   You're then logged in.

If the link says it has expired, just request a new one.

---

## Good to know

- **The website still works if the editing system is down.** It falls back to the last saved
  copy, and the Events page shows a small note saying so.
- **Who can edit?** Only people the church administrator has invited. Nobody can sign themselves up.
- **The morning email.** Each morning (7–8am) a summary is emailed to the address the administrator
  chose. It lists the cancellations and service changes for the next two weeks, upcoming meetings,
  **what** was changed on the admin page in the last 24 hours (never who), yesterday's visitor
  numbers, and a health check of the website. If the subject starts with **PROBLEM**, something
  needs attention. The problems are listed at the top, so pass the email on to the administrator.
  (For the administrator: the email settings are the GitHub secrets and variables listed in
  README.md, including `GOATCOUNTER_TOKEN`, `GOATCOUNTER_CODE` and the optional `SITE_URL`.)
- **Something looks wrong?** Contact the church administrator at admin@tcfmelbourne.org.
